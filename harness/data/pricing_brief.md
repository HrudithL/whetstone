# Brief: Marrow — pricing cards

A three-tier pricing row for the same product (Marrow, a test-coverage-mapping CLI). Build just the
pricing section: a row of three cards plus a short section heading.

**Section heading:** "Pricing"
**Section subtext:** "Start free. Upgrade when your team does."

## Tiers

1. **Solo** — $0 / forever
   - 1 developer
   - Local test maps
   - Community support
   - CTA: "Start free"

2. **Team** — $12 / user / month  *(mark this card as the recommended tier)*
   - Everything in Solo
   - Shared team maps
   - CI integration
   - Email support
   - CTA: "Start 14-day trial"

3. **Enterprise** — Custom
   - Everything in Team
   - SSO / SAML
   - Self-hosted option
   - Dedicated support
   - CTA: "Contact sales"
